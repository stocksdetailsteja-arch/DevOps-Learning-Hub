# Day 09 - Interview Questions

## Basic

### Q1 What Was Created In This Terraform Project?

VPC, Subnets, Security Groups, EC2, S3, Outputs.

### Q2 Why Use Modules?

To increase reusability and reduce duplication.

### Q3 Why Use Outputs?

To expose infrastructure information.

### Q4 Why Use Remote State?

For collaboration and state protection.

## Intermediate

### Q1 Why Separate Infrastructure Into Modules?

Improves maintainability and scalability.

### Q2 Why Validate Infrastructure?

Ensures successful deployment.

### Q3 Why Use Backends?

Store state centrally and securely.
