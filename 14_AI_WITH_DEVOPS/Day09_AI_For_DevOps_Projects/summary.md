## Production Principles

- Facts must be separated from hypotheses.
- Root causes must be verified.
- Secrets must be removed before submission.
- AI output must not automatically control production.
- Destructive actions require explicit approval.
- Every recommendation must include validation.
- Recovery must be confirmed.
- Limitations must be documented honestly.
