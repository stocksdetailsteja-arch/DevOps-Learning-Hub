# AI And DevOps Interview Questions

## What Is AI?

Artificial Intelligence enables systems to perform tasks normally requiring human intelligence.

## What Is Generative AI?

AI capable of producing new content.

Examples:

- Text
- Code
- Images

## What Is An LLM?

Large Language Model.

## How Can AI Help DevOps?

- Automation
- Documentation
- Infrastructure Generation
- Monitoring Analysis
- Troubleshooting

## Can AI Replace DevOps Engineers?

No.

AI assists engineers but human review remains essential.

## What Are Risks Of AI Generated Code?

- Security Flaws
- Incorrect Logic
- Inefficient Design
- Wrong Permissions
