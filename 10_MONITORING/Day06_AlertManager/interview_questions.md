## Beginner Level

### What is AlertManager?

AlertManager is the component that manages alerts generated by Prometheus. 【1-ca89f6】【2-8acb73】

### What does AlertManager do?

- Routing
- Grouping
- Deduplication
- Silencing
- Notification Delivery

### What is Alert Routing?

Sending alerts to the appropriate receiver. 【1-ca89f6】【3-fde8c0】

### What is Alert Grouping?

Combining similar alerts into a single notification. 【1-ca89f6】【4-56ff87】

### Which port does AlertManager use?

9093
