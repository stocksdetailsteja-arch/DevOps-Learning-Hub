# Day 01 - Interview Questions

## Beginner Level

### What is Monitoring?

Monitoring is the process of collecting and analyzing system and application health data.

### Why is Monitoring important?

To detect issues and maintain reliability.

### What are Metrics?

Numerical values measuring system performance.

### What are Logs?

Records of events generated by systems and applications.

### What are Traces?

Request journeys across multiple services.

## Intermediate Level

### Difference between Monitoring and Observability?

Monitoring detects known problems.

Observability helps investigate unknown problems.

### What are the three pillars of Observability?

- Metrics
- Logs
- Traces

### What is an SLI?

Service Level Indicator.

### What is an SLO?

Service Level Objective.

### What is an SLA?

Service Level Agreement.

## Advanced Level (5+ Years)

### Explain RED Method.

- Rate
- Errors
- Duration

Used for application monitoring.

### Explain USE Method.

- Utilization
- Saturation
- Errors

Used for infrastructure monitoring.

### What causes alert fatigue?

Too many low-value alerts causing engineers to ignore important alerts.

### How do you design effective monitoring?

- Monitor user impact
- Create actionable alerts
- Reduce noise
- Use dashboards
