# Day 04 - Lab Notes

## Objective

Learn Kubernetes Deployments and ReplicaSets.

## Services Used

- Deployment

- ReplicaSet

- Pods

## Observations

- Deployments manage Pods.

- ReplicaSets maintain availability.

- Scaling is simple.

- Rollbacks improve reliability.

## Results

Successfully created and managed Deployments.

## Lessons Learned

- Deployments are preferred over direct Pod creation.

- Scaling improves availability.

- Rollbacks reduce deployment risk.
