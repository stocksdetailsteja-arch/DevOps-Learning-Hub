# Day 05 - Lab Notes

## Objective

Learn Kubernetes Services and Service Networking.

## Services Used

- Services

- DNS

- Endpoints

## Observations

- Services provide stable access.

- DNS simplifies service discovery.

- Endpoints map traffic to Pods.

- Services support load balancing.

## Results

Successfully created and managed Kubernetes Services.

## Lessons Learned

- Never access Pods directly in production.

- Services provide durability.

- DNS is critical for microservices.
