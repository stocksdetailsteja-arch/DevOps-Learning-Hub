# Day 10 - Mock Interview And STAR Stories

## Objective

Prepare for complete DevOps interview rounds including:

- HR Round
- Technical Round
- Scenario Round
- Troubleshooting Round
- Managerial Round
- Client Round

## Why This Topic Matters

Many engineers know technology but struggle to:

- Explain projects
- Answer behavioral questions
- Demonstrate leadership
- Communicate production incidents
- Explain troubleshooting approaches

## Learning Outcomes

After completing this module you should be able to:

- Introduce yourself confidently
- Explain projects effectively
- Answer behavioral questions
- Answer production scenario questions
- Present STAR stories
- Handle difficult interview situations

## Interview Areas Covered

### HR Round

- Career Goals
