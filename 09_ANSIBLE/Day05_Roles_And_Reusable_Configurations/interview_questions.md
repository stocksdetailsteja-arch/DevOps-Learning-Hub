# Day 05 - Interview Questions

## Beginner Level

### What is an Ansible Role?

An Ansible Role is a structured collection of tasks, handlers, variables and templates used for automation.

### Why are Roles used?

Roles improve:

- Reusability
- Readability
- Maintainability

### What are the main folders inside a Role?

- tasks
- handlers
- templates
- files
- vars
- defaults
- meta

### What is tasks/main.yml?

It contains the primary tasks executed by the Role.
